## 1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

"Confidential Information" includes, but is not limited to:

a) **Source Code** — All software code, scripts, configurations, database schemas, and technical documentation related to the Garment ERP system and any associated applications.

b) **Business Information** — Customer lists, supplier details, pricing structures, financial data, business strategies, and operational processes.

c) **Technical Information** — System architecture, algorithms, APIs, integrations, deployment configurations, credentials, and security measures.

d) **Data** — Any data processed by or stored in the systems, including but not limited to customer data, order data, inventory data, and financial records.

e) **Documentation** — All written materials, guides, specifications, and communications related to the project.

---

## 2. OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY

The Receiving Party agrees to:

a) **Maintain Confidentiality** — Keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential and not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent from the Disclosing Party.

b) **Limited Use** — Use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of providing development services to the Disclosing Party as agreed.

c) **No Copying** — Not copy, reproduce, or duplicate any Confidential Information except as necessary to perform the agreed services.

d) **No Reverse Engineering** — Not reverse engineer, decompile, or derive source code from any compiled software provided.

e) **Secure Storage** — Store any Confidential Information securely and take reasonable measures to prevent unauthorized access.

f) **Return or Destroy** — Upon termination of engagement or upon request, promptly return or destroy all copies of Confidential Information in their possession.

g) **No Competing Product** — Not use Confidential Information to develop, market, or sell any product or service that competes with the Disclosing Party's business for a period of **24 months** after termination.

## 3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

a) All work product, code, designs, and materials created by the Receiving Party during this engagement shall be the **sole property of the Disclosing Party**.

b) The Receiving Party **assigns all rights**, including copyright, patent rights, and trade secrets, to the Disclosing Party.

c) The Receiving Party **waives all moral rights** to the work product to the extent permitted by law.

d) The Receiving Party shall not claim any ownership or rights to the Confidential Information or any derivative works.

---

## 4. EXCLUSIONS

This Agreement does not apply to information that:

a) Was publicly available at the time of disclosure, or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party.

b) Was already known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure, as evidenced by written records.

c) Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of Confidential Information.

d) Is disclosed pursuant to a court order or legal requirement, provided the Receiving Party gives prompt notice to the Disclosing Party.

---

## 5. TERM AND TERMINATION

a) This Agreement is effective from the Effective Date and continues for **3 years** after the termination of the business relationship.

b) The confidentiality obligations for trade secrets shall continue indefinitely.

c) Either party may terminate the development engagement with **14 days** written notice, but confidentiality obligations survive termination.

## 6. REMEDIES

a) The Receiving Party acknowledges that breach of this Agreement may cause **irreparable harm** to the Disclosing Party.

b) The Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek **injunctive relief** in addition to any other legal remedies.

c) The Receiving Party agrees to pay all **legal costs and damages** incurred by the Disclosing Party in enforcing this Agreement.

d) **Liquidated Damages:** In the event of breach, the Receiving Party agrees to pay a minimum of **INR 10,00,000 (Ten Lakhs)** as liquidated damages, in addition to actual damages.
